\section{Precision}
\label{sec:precision}

\begin{question}
  \textbf{What do FP8 and MXFP8 buy, and do they train the same?} Low precision doubles the matmul rate
  but not the network: does the gain measured on one node survive at 52 nodes? Does the loss follow BF16?
\end{question}

\paragraph{Speed (C-speed).} Recipes: BF16 and MXFP8 for Qwen3.8-27B (the pinned TorchTitan has no other
FP8 recipe); BF16, FP8 tensorwise, FP8 blockwise and MXFP8 for GLM-5. Each runs on the best layout, at 1
node (Qwen) or 13 nodes (GLM-5), and at 52 nodes. For Qwen, \TriQwenFpBestRecipe{} speeds up training by
$\times$\TriQwenFpSpeedupSmall{} on 1 node and $\times$\TriQwenFpSpeedupFull{} on 52 nodes. For GLM-5, the
best recipe, \TriGlmFpBestRecipe, gives $\times$\TriGlmFpSpeedupSmall{} on 13 nodes and
$\times$\TriGlmFpSpeedupFull{} on 52 nodes.

\paragraph{Loss (C-loss).} The same recipes train for 500 steps at 13 nodes, from the same seed, on the
same FineWeb data, with a realistic learning rate. The difference to the BF16 loss at each step measures
the effect of the recipe.
