\section{Precision}
\label{sec:precision}

\begin{question}
  \textbf{What do FP8 and MXFP8 buy, and do they train the same?} Low precision doubles the matmul rate
  but not the network: does the gain measured on one node survive at 52 nodes? Does the loss follow BF16?
\end{question}

\paragraph{Speed (C-speed).} Recipes: BF16; FP8 with tensorwise scaling; FP8 with rowwise (Qwen) or
blockwise (GLM-5) scaling; MXFP8. Each runs on the best layout, at 1 node (Qwen) or 13 nodes (GLM-5),
and at 52 nodes.
